DharmApp Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026 · Contact: ashisgoyal@gmail.com

DharmApp ("we", "the app") is a devotional app offering daily messages, darshan images, bhajans, puja vidhi, panchang, sacred texts and a Guru community. We collect as little as possible.

1. Data stored only on your device

  • Language, theme, text size, Ishta Devta, chosen city and reminder time.
  • Favourites, playlists, listening history, bookmarks, reading progress and mala-jaap counts.

This data never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app or using Settings → Delete my account & data removes it.

2. Location (optional)

If you allow it, approximate location is used on the device to calculate sunrise, sunset, Rahu Kaal and tithi. It is not sent to us or anyone else. You can instead pick a city manually.

3. Photos (optional)

When you choose a photo for a greeting card or a Guru post, the app reads only that photo. Greeting cards are created on your device. Saving to your gallery asks for permission first.

4. Guru community (only if you sign in)

If you sign in with your email to follow gurus, like posts or register as a Guru, we store your email address, profile (name, place, bio, photo), posts, follows, likes and reports with our backend provider Supabase. Guru profiles and posts are public inside the app. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete my account & data, or by emailing us.

5. Notifications

Daily message and festival reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not run a push-notification server.

6. Third-party content services

The app loads content directly from these services, which receive your IP address as part of normal internet requests and apply their own privacy policies:

  • YouTube (embedded player) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Internet Archive (audio) — archive.org terms
  • Radio Browser (radio directory) and the radio stations you play
  • Wikimedia Commons (images) — Wikimedia privacy policy
  • vedicscriptures.github.io (Gita) and Project Gutenberg (books)

7. Advertising & analytics

The current version contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics.

8. Children

DharmApp is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. The Guru community requires sign-in by an adult.

9. Changes

We will update this page and the date above if this policy changes.

10. How to delete your DharmApp account and data

In the app: open Settings → Delete my account & data and confirm. This immediately deletes your account, email, Guru profile, posts, follows, likes and reports from our servers, and clears all app data on your phone.

Without the app: email ashisgoyal@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my DharmApp account”. We delete the account and all the data listed above within 7 days and reply to confirm.

We keep nothing after deletion, except where the law requires us to keep a record of abuse reports for up to 90 days.

11. Community guidelines (Guru section)

By registering as a Guru or posting, you agree to these rules. Breaking them gets content removed and accounts banned.

  • Post only respectful devotional, spiritual or educational content that you own or have the right to share.
  • No hate speech or insults against any religion, caste, community, gender or person.
  • No nudity, violence, threats, harassment or bullying.
  • No scams, fake miracles or cures, requests for money or donations through the app, or misleading health claims.
  • No impersonating another guru, temple or organisation.
  • No spam, advertising or links to harmful sites.

Every user can report a post or block a Guru from the ⋮ menu. Posts reported by several users are hidden automatically and reviewed by us, normally within 24 hours. Every new Guru profile is reviewed before it appears publicly. To report something urgent, email ashisgoyal@gmail.com.
